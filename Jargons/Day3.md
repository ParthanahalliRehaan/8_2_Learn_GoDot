# D1 : 📘 Game Design Terms

- **BDD (Blackout Design Document)**  
  - A design document specifically created for the **Blackout version of a game**.  
  - Functions like a GDD but tailored to that particular version’s mechanics, features, and scope.

- **Arcade**  
  - A large room or venue filled with coin-operated machines.  
  - Players insert coins to play **short, fast-paced games**.  
  - Classic social gaming spaces before home consoles became widespread.
